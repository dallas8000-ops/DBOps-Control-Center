# Support SLA and Response Matrix

## Support Channels
- Primary: email ticket queue
- Secondary: shared chat channel
- Escalation: on-call phone bridge (Premium only)

## Support Hours
- Essential: Mon-Fri, 9:00 to 17:00 customer local time
- Standard: Mon-Sat, 8:00 to 20:00 customer local time
- Premium: 24x7

## Severity Definitions
- Sev 1: production outage or critical data access loss
- Sev 2: major feature unavailable with no practical workaround
- Sev 3: degraded behavior with workaround available
- Sev 4: minor defect, cosmetic issue, or advisory question

## Response Targets
| Severity | Essential | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | 4 business hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| Sev 2 | 8 business hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| Sev 3 | 2 business days | 1 business day | 8 hours |
| Sev 4 | 5 business days | 3 business days | 1 business day |

## Update Cadence During Active Incidents
- Sev 1: every 30 minutes (Premium) or every 60 minutes (Essential/Standard)
- Sev 2: every 4 hours
- Sev 3: daily business update
- Sev 4: updates per scheduled maintenance cycle

## Scope Boundaries
- SLA applies to deployed application and approved integrations only.
- SLA excludes third-party platform outages outside direct control.
- SLA excludes enhancement requests and roadmap feature development.
